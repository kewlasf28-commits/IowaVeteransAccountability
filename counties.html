Understanding Iowa's 99 Counties

Iowa has 99 counties, each with veterans who may qualify for federal benefits, healthcare, housing assistance, and other services.

This research project examines the geographic distribution of veterans and VA benefits across Iowa.

Our goal: Determine how many veterans are estimated to live in each county, how much VA funding is attributed to each county, and what information is available about veterans who are not receiving assistance.

Where Will the Data Come From?

County-level information will be gathered from publicly available sources, including:

  • VA Geographic Distribution of Expenditures
  • VA Veteran Population Estimates
  • VA Office of Inspector General
  • U.S. Census Bureau
Research standard: All published figures should identify their reporting year and original source. Estimates, actual expenditures, and verified individual payments must not be confused.
