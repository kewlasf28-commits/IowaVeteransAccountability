Understanding Iowa's 99 Counties

Iowa has 99 counties, each with veterans who may qualify for disability compensation, healthcare, education, housing assistance, and other federal benefits.

Understanding how these benefits are distributed requires examining veteran population estimates, actual government expenditures, and the availability of assistance programs.

Our Mission: Determine how many veterans are estimated to live in each county, how much federal funding is attributed to each county, and whether veterans who need assistance can access available programs.

This project begins with Polk, Dallas, Story, and Linn counties. Additional counties will be added as information is verified.

Questions About County-Level Accountability

Public financial records provide important information about spending, but additional records are necessary to determine how effectively programs serve veterans.

  1. How much disability compensation is paid directly to veterans residing in each Iowa county?
  2. How much money is spent on VA employees, administration, and contracted services?
  3. How many veterans in each county experience homelessness during an entire year?
  4. How many veterans request housing assistance but do not receive it?
  5. How many benefits claims are denied, delayed, or overturned on appeal?
  6. What independent audits have identified financial errors or weaknesses affecting veterans in Iowa?
  7. What improvements have resulted from government investigations and recommendations?
Research Principle: Financial transparency requires distinguishing documented facts, statistical estimates, unresolved questions, and confirmed wrongdoing.
