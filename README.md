# Iowa Veterans Accountability 🇺🇸

### Follow the Money. Examine the Evidence. Demand Transparency.

## About This Project

Iowa Veterans Accountability is an independent public-interest research website dedicated to examining government spending, veterans' benefits, homelessness, and financial accountability in Iowa.

The purpose of this project is to provide accessible information about how public resources are allocated and whether veterans are receiving the services and benefits they need.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to promote transparency, responsible government spending, and accountability in programs serving Iowa veterans.

We believe public spending should be evaluated not only by the amount of money distributed but also by the results achieved for veterans.

## Website Features

- **Home Page:** Overview of Iowa veterans' benefits, spending, homelessness, and federal oversight findings.
- **Iowa Counties:** County-level veteran population estimates and available VA spending information.
- **Follow the Money:** Research distinguishing direct veteran benefits from healthcare, administration, and employee expenses.
- **Investigations:** Documented findings from federal oversight agencies.
- **Sources:** Links to government reports and public datasets.

## Research Sources

This project uses publicly available information from:

- U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)
- VA Office of Inspector General (OIG)
- U.S. Government Accountability Office (GAO)
- U.S. Census Bureau
- Federal government financial and workforce reports

Figures are identified by reporting year where available. Statistical estimates, reported expenditures, and confirmed investigative findings are distinguished from unverified allegations.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent research project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any government agency.

The information is provided for public education and research. The existence of improper payments or administrative errors does not, by itself, establish fraud.
